## Cross-job scope concerns

- The formal set is property-scoped and mostly bounded. It does not compose the whole Fabric or establish unrestricted AXI4 compliance.
- Several channel-path harnesses treat legality, decode, ownership, outstanding permission, or busy state as free inputs. Their results do not establish those facts from the integrated design.
- B/R router harnesses assume target-source stability but use an unconstrained busy bitmap rather than a connected outstanding bank. They cannot establish end-to-end response-to-accepted-request association.
- The documented missing recorded-target-versus-returned-target matching remains outside the response-router proofs.
- The QoS harness covers threshold behavior but has no recurring-service assumptions or bounded-service assertion; fairness is not proved there.
